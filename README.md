### Research Question

Which are potential drugs to treat multiple sclerosis (MS) and what are their main mechanism of action? 


### Visualisation Method

In this project, I am aiming to visualise different classes of drugs that are currently used to treat MS and their respective drugs in an interactive web of network. 

#### Potential followup question

Which genes are associated with MS and how do these relate to the drugs listed already? 
This question mainly concerns the developed monoclonal antibodies, and I would like to additionally show how they specifically target risk genes associated with MS, if the time allows.
